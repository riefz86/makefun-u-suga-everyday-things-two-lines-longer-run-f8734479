Two lines about a traffic jam, built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run” bounty, $2 in USDC.

The lines:

> A traffic jam is like a school hallway at lunch, with everyone trying to squeeze through one narrow doorway at once, so the whole crowd shuffles forward one tiny step at a time.
> It shows how one tight opening can slow a whole crowd down, but it leaves out that cars cannot turn sideways or slip past each other the way people can.

Line 1 explains a traffic jam to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence built on an analogy they can picture — a crowded school hallway squeezing through one doorway. Line 2 says why the analogy works and where it stops: it maps the way a tight opening slows a whole crowd, but leaves out that cars cannot turn sideways or slip past each other the way people can.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
